Who is IDR?

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) stands at the forefront of the technology risk landscape, functioning as an elite, industry-led training and deployment institute.

We specially cultivate top-tier talent for critical digital, cyber, and technology risk roles tailored for heavily regulated sectors. Our approach is rooted in what the industry demands. We seamlessly integrate rigorous academic insight with hands-on, real-world project execution.
